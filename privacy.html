Chromatic 2048 Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy describes how AlexVentor (“we”) collects, uses and shares information when you use the app Chromatic 2048 (“the app” or “the game”). By using the app you accept this policy.

1. Information we collect

Chromatic 2048 does not require creating an account or signing up with your email. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, or access to your contacts, photos or microphone. This is the data that is processed:

DataWhat we use it forHow it is handled
Game data saved on your device (settings, saved run, records)To run the game and save your progressStored encrypted on your device only; they are not sent to our servers.
Player ID and public name of your Google Play Games accountOnline leaderboards and showing your name in the tableShared with Google Play Games Services.
Device advertising ID, approximate IP address, device data (model, OS, language) and ad interactionsTo show, measure and cap the frequency of ads; to prevent fraudShared with Google AdMob and its advertising partners.
Your purchase status (product, purchase token). We do not receive your payment details.To unlock the full game / remove ads and to restore your purchasePayment is processed by the store (Google Play / App Store).

2. How we use information

  • To provide and maintain the game and save your progress.
  • To show online leaderboards.
  • To show and measure ads.
  • To manage your purchases and restore them.
  • To prevent fraud and cheating, and to meet legal obligations.

3. Who we share information with

Chromatic 2048 uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or for their own purposes. These are the third parties data is shared with:

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share data with any other third parties (for example data brokers or social networks) except where required by law.

Advertising

The free version shows interstitial ads between games through Google AdMob. Ads may or may not be personalized, depending on your settings and consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent (Google consent form, UMP) before showing personalized ads, and you can change it later. You can reset or hide your advertising ID from Android settings (Google > Ads). If you buy the ad removal, no ads are shown.

4. Storage and security

Local data (settings, saved run and records) is stored encrypted on your device. We use secure connections (HTTPS) when communicating with third-party services. No method is 100 % foolproof, but we apply reasonable measures to protect you.

5. Retention and deletion of data

Local data remains while the game is installed; it is deleted when you uninstall it. To delete your Google Play Games data (scores and player profile) use the Play Games settings or email us and we will make the corresponding request. You can ask us to delete any data linked to you by writing to melerobits@gmail.com. More details in Account and data deletion.

6. Your rights

Depending on your country (for example GDPR in the EU/EEA, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA in California, LGPD in Brazil) you may access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, withdraw your consent and complain to your data protection authority. Write to us and we will reply within a reasonable time.

7. International transfers

The third parties listed may process data in countries other than yours, applying the appropriate legal safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses).

Children

Chromatic 2048 is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, email melerobits@gmail.com and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will publish the current version at this same address with its update date; if the changes are important, we will let you know inside the app.
